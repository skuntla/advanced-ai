# Fourteen enterprise AI solution families

Each design family is a distinct enterprise task class, not a specific framework. Use the chapter links to practice system architecture and trade-offs.

- **S01** [Knowledge and search assistants](01-knowledge-and-search-assistants/README.md) — Employees ask natural-language questions about internal manuals, policies and research.
- **S02** [Document intelligence](02-document-intelligence/README.md) — Extract clauses, tables and events from contracts, invoices and disclosures.
- **S03** [Agentic business automation](03-agentic-business-automation/README.md) — Investigate and resolve exceptions across several enterprise applications.
- **S04** [Customer-facing conversational AI](04-customer-facing-conversational-ai/README.md) — Customer support chatbot, omnichannel account assistant and voice contact center.
- **S05** [Decision intelligence and analytics](05-decision-intelligence-and-analytics/README.md) — Financial research, text-to-SQL, BI copilots and explainable decision support.
- **S06** [Predictive AI and anomaly detection](06-predictive-ai-and-anomaly-detection/README.md) — Fraud scoring, reconciliation anomaly detection, forecasting and risk models.
- **S07** [Recommendation and personalization](07-recommendation-and-personalization/README.md) — Next-best product, search ranking and personalization across channels.
- **S08** [Software engineering agents](08-software-engineering-agents/README.md) — Code generation, refactoring, migrations and test-automation systems.
- **S09** [AI operations and cybersecurity agents](09-ai-operations-and-cybersecurity-agents/README.md) — Log investigation, alert correlation, incident triage and guarded remediation.
- **S10** [Multimodal AI applications](10-multimodal-ai-applications/README.md) — Document vision, call transcription, video analysis and quality inspection.
- **S11** [Enterprise AI platforms](11-enterprise-ai-platforms/README.md) — Internal multi-tenant model gateways, agent runtime, evaluation and policy services.
- **S12** [Custom AI model systems](12-custom-ai-model-systems/README.md) — Fine-tuned compliance classifier, enterprise embeddings and self-hosted model serving.
- **S13** [Real-time and edge AI](13-real-time-and-edge-ai/README.md) — Factory vision, low-latency sensor analytics and on-device intelligence.
- **S14** [Optimization and planning systems](14-optimization-and-planning-systems/README.md) — Workforce allocation, supply-chain optimization, scheduling and constraints.

Study each using the [solution-design template](../00-start-here/TEMPLATES.md), cross-reference the [27 domains](../00-start-here/CURRICULUM-MAP.md) and test against the [14 failure categories](../03-failure-categories/README.md).
