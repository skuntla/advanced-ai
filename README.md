# Advanced AI — Enterprise AI Architect Mastery

**Canonical, versioned learning plan** · Established 2026-10-24 · Owner-directed, AI-native, evidence-driven.

This repository is the long-term home for preparing toward **Senior/Staff/Principal AI Engineer, Enterprise AI Solutions Architect and Forward-Deployed Engineer** positions. It is intentionally broader than RAG, fine-tuning or any single framework.

## The locked taxonomy
- **7 pillars / 27 knowledge domains** — mechanisms you need to understand and defend.
- **14 solution families** — systems an enterprise might ask you to design.
- **14 failure categories** — what can go wrong and what you must contain, diagnose, fix or recover from.

**Locked** means these 55 indexed areas and their IDs remain stable. Extend subtopics and references as the field evolves; record intentional taxonomy changes in [CHANGELOG](00-start-here/CHANGELOG.md). No framework, benchmark or vendor is permanently endorsed.

## Working principles
- Own the **outcome**, not merely a diagram or an AI-generated codebase.
- Provide AI agents full business/technical context, ask them to challenge assumptions and surface failure modes, interrogate the design, then authorize implementation.
- Model confidence and a convincing explanation are **not proof**. Require independent tests, observable traces and domain-owner sign-off.
- Prefer simpler workflows to autonomous agents until dynamic behavior creates measurable value.
- Keep regulated/financial work inside synthetic data and isolated labs unless organizational approvals explicitly authorize real data.
- Track factual claims and resource URLs; source materials change.
