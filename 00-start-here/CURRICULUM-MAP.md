## Why this isn't a list of tools
RAG, chunking and fine-tuning are mechanisms. Enterprises additionally require identity, process design, data contracts, transactional correctness, system operations, observability, governance, human authority and economic justification. Many important systems use predictive ML, deterministic automation, multimodal inference or optimization instead of RAG.
